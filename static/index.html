How it works

STEP 01

Web Search

Queries Serper (Google Search) and collects the top results for your topic.

STEP 02

Scrape

Fetches each page concurrently and extracts the main content, stripping ads and boilerplate.

STEP 03

LLM Extract

An LLM reads each page and pulls out entities with attributes — plus a verbatim quote per value as evidence.

STEP 04

Merge

Entities found across multiple pages are deduplicated and merged, keeping all source citations.

Built with FastAPI · Serper · OpenRouter · trafilatura  ·  Every cell is traceable to its source.  ·  Hosted on Render free tier — first load after inactivity may take ~30s.
